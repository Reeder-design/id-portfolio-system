Safety notes

Before you paste source material

Good fit

Public-safe drafts, generalized project notes, portfolio copy, sanitized excerpts, or information you are authorized to send.

Do not send

Passwords, credentials, private keys, customer secrets, restricted internal material, or proprietary/unreleased details you are not authorized to share with an external AI service.

Sanitize mode

Helps draft a generalized public version, but it does not make confidential input safe to transmit in the first place and it does not guarantee publication safety.

Evidence rule

The assistant is instructed not to invent metrics, tools, accomplishments, clients, products, or outcomes that are not supported by your source.
