About Me

Learning design, development, and the systems around it

I design enterprise learning for sales and partner audiences, especially when the subject is technical or product-heavy. My work also reaches into LMS operations, reporting, automation, AI evaluation, and custom digital content when those pieces are part of the learning problem.

Before corporate L&D, I taught high school history and English. That classroom experience and my Master of Education still shape how I think about practice, feedback, assessment, and whether learning actually changes performance.
