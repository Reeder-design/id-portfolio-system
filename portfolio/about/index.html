About Me

Learning designed for clarity, usefulness, and performance.

I've worked in various fields creating instructional design content. From public to private sectors, adults to children, humans to bots, I can design for all. My most recent work has focused around building content for enterprise learning environments for audiences such as sales and partner enablement, product launch support, and customer support. My projects range from interactive learning and certification pathways to LMS processes, analytics, workflow automation and project management, and custom digital content.

Before moving into corporate L&D, I taught high school history and english. That classroom background, along with my Master of Education, still shapes how I think about clarity, practice, feedback, assessment, and whether learning actually supports performance.

Learning Needs

The nuance of learning needs.

My job isn't to turn every piece of SME knowledge into training. It's to figure out what the learner actually needs and organize it in a way that makes sense.

From the learning need, I can determine the most effective learning solution to pursue— whether that's a course, scenario, resource, pathway, performance support, or something else entirely.

Start with the performance need. Then decide what learning actually belongs there.
