How I adapt the work to the learning need

Instructional Design for eLearning

Build the shortest path from need to performance.

The audience, task, complexity, source material, delivery environment, and maintenance needs determine whether the solution becomes a course, scenario, multimedia asset, assessment, reference tool, or connected pathway.

What I optimize for

Relevance Transfer Accessibility Maintainability

How the workflow changes

01

Understand the need

Define the audience, performance gap, business context, constraints, and what success should look like.

02

Align the solution

Translate the need into objectives, evidence of performance, scope, source-content requirements, and the right delivery format.

03

Design the experience

Plan structure, practice, assessment, storyboards, scripts, prompts, visuals, and reusable assets before heavy development.

04

Develop and validate

Build efficiently, review with SMEs, test accessibility and functionality, complete QA, and confirm the experience works in its delivery environment.

05

Launch and improve

Measure use and outcomes, collect feedback, document ownership, and keep the content editable enough to update instead of rebuild.

What good looks like

A clear, relevant experience that gives learners useful practice, supports the required performance, survives review and QA, and can be maintained after launch.
