About Me

Learning design, development, and the systems around it

I've designed learning across education, corporate L&D, technical and commercial enablement, and AI evaluation. My recent work focuses on enterprise learning for sales and partners, product launches, technical foundations, and customer support, alongside LMS operations, analytics, workflow automation, project management, and custom digital content.

Learning principles I use

I start with the performance need, design for useful practice and feedback, and build measurement and maintainability into the solution from the beginning.

Performance first

Define what someone needs to do before deciding what training should exist.

Practice with purpose

Use realistic decisions, examples, and feedback instead of interaction for its own sake.

Evidence matters

Connect assessments, analytics, feedback, and business context to improvement decisions.

Design for ownership

Build content, documentation, and workflows so they can be maintained and reused.
