Background

Hi there,

I started in high-school classrooms and K-12 curriculum, where Universal Design, the Zone of Proximal Development, and problem-posing education shaped how I think about access, challenge, and learner agency. I now design enterprise learning for adult audiences across eLearning, interactive enablement, facilitation, LMS operations, AI quality evaluation, web and coding work, AI chat experiences, and automated workflows.
