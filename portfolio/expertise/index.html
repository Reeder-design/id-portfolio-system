Common Hiring Questions

Quick answers to the things people usually want to know.

What kind of instructional designer are you?

I am strongest in enterprise learning for complex products and systems, especially sales and partner enablement. I can design the learning itself and also work across LMS operations, reporting, automation, multimedia, AI evaluation, and launch support when the project needs it.

Can you design technical training without being the engineer?

Yes. I work with technical source material and SMEs to identify what the audience actually needs to understand or do, then translate that into role-appropriate explanations, comparisons, scenarios, practice, and decision support without pretending the learner needs engineering-level depth.

Do you only work in Articulate?

No. Storyline and Rise are core authoring tools for me, but the workflow matters more than the interface. I also work across Adobe Creative Cloud, Captivate, LMS platforms, Figma-style prototyping, Python automation, HTML/CSS/JavaScript, reporting tools, and enterprise collaboration systems.

How do you decide whether something should be training?

I look for the actual performance gap first. If the issue is process, access, ownership, documentation, incentives, or system friction, I would rather recommend a workflow change, job aid, or support resource than build a course that cannot solve the problem.

How do you use AI in instructional design?

I use it for structured acceleration, not unchecked generation. That can include evaluation, source-grounded drafting, agent instructions, reusable workflow support, rapid prototyping, and QA, with clear constraints and human review built into the process.
