Original Task Create a five-minute microlearning lesson for newly hired sales representatives introducing a fictional networking product called AtlasLink. Include: a measurable learning objective, three key selling points, and one scenario-based knowledge check with feedback.

AI-Generated Draft

Introducing AtlasLink

AtlasLink is a next-generation networking solution designed to help organizations modernize their business infrastructure.

Learning Objective

By the end of this lesson, learners will understand AtlasLink and why customers should buy it.

Three Key Selling Points

  • AtlasLink reduces network operating costs by up to 60%.
  • AtlasLink provides fast, reliable connectivity for modern organizations.
  • AtlasLink is easy to deploy and works for virtually every type of customer.

Customer Scenario

A customer tells you they want a better network. What should you recommend?

A. AtlasLink
B. Nothing
C. Tell the customer networking is not important

Correct answer: A

Feedback: Great job! AtlasLink is the best choice because it provides modern networking capabilities.

Compare with my evaluation

My benchmark score for this fictional artifact is 60%, or Major Revision.

Accuracy

The 60% cost-reduction claim is presented as fact even though the prompt provides no source supporting it. “Works for virtually every customer” is also an unjustified absolute claim.

Instructional Quality

“Understand AtlasLink” is not a measurable objective. The knowledge check is also too obvious to measure meaningful application, and its feedback does not explain the reasoning.

Alignment & Completeness

The output technically includes the requested components, but several are weakly executed. Meeting the format of a request is not the same as meeting its intended purpose.

Overall Recommendation

Keep the basic structure, but revise the unsupported claims, rewrite the objective, create a realistic customer scenario, and provide feedback that teaches the reasoning behind the decision.
