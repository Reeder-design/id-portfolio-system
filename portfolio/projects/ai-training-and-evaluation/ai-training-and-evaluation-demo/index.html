What needs attention?

Select all issue categories that are materially supported by the evidence. Then classify the highest-severity problem and write a concise evaluator note.

1. Identify the issue categories.

Select only categories you can support from the task, source information, and output.

2. Classify the highest issue severity.

Consider what would happen if the artifact reached its intended human user without revision.

3. Write actionable evaluator feedback.

Identify the problem, explain why it matters, and describe what a stronger output should do.

A short, specific note is enough. Aim for at least 20 characters.
