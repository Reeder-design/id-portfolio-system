What This Is

A small output-evaluation exercise.

This demo shows how I compare an AI response with the assignment and approved source before I judge quality. The cases are fictional and designed to demonstrate the review process without using client or employer content.

How to Use It

Review the evidence, then make the call.

Choose a case. Read the assignment, approved source, and model output. Use the evidence scan if you want help locating the main problem. Choose the severity that fits the issue, then reveal the benchmark to compare the reasoning.
