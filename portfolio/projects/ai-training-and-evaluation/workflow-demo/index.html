Interactive Workflow

Keep the evidence beside the judgment.

Select a review stage, then use the compact packet to check the task, source, or model output without leaving the decision.

Task

Write a partner-facing product summary.

Identify the ideal customer, include two approved value points, and use only the fictional product brief.

Do not add: savings percentages, uptime guarantees, support commitments, zero-downtime claims, or universal positioning.

Stage 1

Frame the assignment before reading for quality.

Turn the prompt into a short checklist of what the model must do and what it must not do.

Reviewer question: What would make this response succeed or fail the assignment?
