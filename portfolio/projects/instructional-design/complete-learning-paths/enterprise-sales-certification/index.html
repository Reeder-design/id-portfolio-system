What I Did

I built and maintained the learning path from source material through LMS delivery.

The source material was broad, technical, frequently changing, and not all regions needed the same content. My job was to turn it into focused sales learning without drifting into engineering or installation training.

Designed the curriculum

I reviewed approved source material, wrote seller-focused objectives, grouped content into courses, and decided what belonged in the core path, regional learning, or optional foundations.

Built the learning

I developed Rise and Storyline lessons, comparisons, diagrams, scenarios, knowledge checks, multimedia, and assessment content tied to seller decisions.

Reviewed, tested, and maintained it

I coordinated internal QA and SME review, resolved feedback, tested the learner experience in the LMS, supported migration validation, and updated content as approved sources changed.
