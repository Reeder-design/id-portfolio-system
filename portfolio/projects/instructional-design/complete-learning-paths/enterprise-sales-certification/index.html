Project Overview

Turn a complex cellular networking portfolio into usable sales decisions.

The source material was technically dense and changed over time. Sellers needed enough technical context to recognize a customer need and have a credible conversation, but they did not need engineering or deployment training. I built the curriculum around the decisions sellers actually make.

Problem

Too much source detail, not enough seller structure.

I translated approved technical, product, and sales inputs into one pathway with clear role boundaries.

What I owned

Curriculum, build, assessment, review, LMS testing, and maintenance.

I worked from objective writing through Storyline/Rise development, SME review, learner-path validation, and updates.

Public boundary

The real design approach, without confidential content.

Delivery + Results

Review

LMS + UAT

Tested visibility, enrollment, launch behavior, learner routes, renewal behavior, imported history, and regional requirements.
