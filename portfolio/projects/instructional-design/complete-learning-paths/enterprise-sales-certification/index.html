The Challenge

Build one pathway that stays useful across roles, regions, and change.

The learning had to help sellers recognize fit, compare solution paths, ask better discovery questions, and know when to involve a specialist—without turning sales training into technical implementation training.

Mixed experience

Support newcomers without slowing experienced sellers.

Regional requirements

Separate broadly required learning from market-specific extensions.

Living content

Make updates manageable as messaging, naming, and platform rules change.
