Project Scope

The challenge was breadth: technical source material, regional differences, and a changing product portfolio.

The source library mixed sales, product, and technical information, while different learner groups did not need the same depth or regional requirements. I had to turn that material into one maintainable seller pathway without drifting into engineering or installation training.

Curriculum architecture

I wrote seller-focused objectives, grouped source material into courses, and separated core requirements, regional learning, and optional foundations.

Learning development

I built Rise and Storyline lessons, diagrams, comparisons, scenarios, knowledge checks, multimedia, and assessment content around seller decisions.

Release + operations

I coordinated QA and SME review, tested the learner journey in the LMS, supported migration validation and reporting, and maintained the path as approved sources changed.

I separated universal, regional, and optional content instead of making every seller take the same path.
