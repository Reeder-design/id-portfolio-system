01

Need

What needed to change?

Consolidate overlapping legacy training, updated solution messaging, assessment requirements, and partner-facing enablement into one coherent certification experience that could be maintained as the portfolio changed.

Audience

Internal sales teams and external sales partners learning how to position a complex enterprise technology portfolio.

What learners needed to do

  • Explain the solution family in customer and business terms.
  • Connect customer needs and use cases to the appropriate solution.
  • Use realistic customer examples to support value conversations.
  • Demonstrate required knowledge through an objective-aligned certification assessment.
02

Design Decisions

How I shaped the solution

Approach

Treat the certification as a connected system rather than a single course. I mapped legacy content against current objectives, separated must-know sales knowledge from reference material, planned where real customer use cases supported transfer, and connected learning, practice, assessment, LMS behavior, and update ownership.

03

Build

How I developed and validated it

Built the pathway around modular eLearning content, a reusable source and asset library, structured storyboards and review notes, iterative SME validation, and a certification assessment. Development decisions favored reusable components and editable source files so later product, naming, or branding changes could be made without rebuilding the experience.

04

Outcome

What came out of the work?

  • Created a single pathway architecture for content that previously lived across overlapping training.
  • Aligned course content, customer examples, and assessment to the same sales-performance outcomes.
  • Established a reusable source-content, asset, review, and maintenance structure for future updates.
