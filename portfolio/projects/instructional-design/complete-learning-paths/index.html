eLearning Lifecycle

Follow the work from need to next update.

Choose a stage to see what changes before the next handoff.

Stage 1 / Analyze

Find the performance need before choosing the deliverable.

Clarify the audience, business goal, current performance, constraints, source material, and what someone needs to do differently.

Plan

Audience, performance gap, outcomes, constraints, stakeholder roles, source-content inventory, and success criteria.

Decision

Determine whether the need calls for instruction, performance support, a pathway, or a smaller intervention.

Handoff Signal

The problem, audience, scope, and expected performance are clear enough to design against.

The final stage feeds evidence back into the next analysis cycle.

Maintainability

The source files and workflow are part of the deliverable.

Reusable templates, organized assets, documented decisions, editable source files, clear ownership, and feedback make the next update faster and safer.
