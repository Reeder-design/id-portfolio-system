Systems-Level Learning Design

Design the learner journey, not just the course.

A complete learning pathway brings multiple learning components together around a defined performance goal. Learners need to know what to complete, in what order, how progress is measured, and what successful completion means.

Behind that experience is a larger system: curriculum structure, content dependencies, assessments, LMS configuration, certification requirements, learner communications, reporting, and feedback loops.

Typical Components

One pathway, multiple systems.

  • Audience + learning needs
  • Curriculum architecture
  • Course sequence
  • Practice + assessment

  • LMS configuration
  • Completion tracking
  • Reporting + continuous improvement

Learning Pathway Model

Connect learning to performance over time.

I approach a complete pathway as a connected system rather than a collection of courses.

1

Define the Performance Need

Identify the audience, business goal, current-state gaps, and expected performance.

2

Architect the Pathway

Determine what learners need, the right sequence, prerequisites, learning formats, and progression logic.

3

Build + Assess

Develop the content, practice, resources, and assessments needed to support the pathway.

4

Deliver Through the LMS

Configure the learner journey, enrollments, requirements, completions, certifications, and supporting communications.

5

Measure + Improve

Use completion data, assessment results, learner feedback, and reporting to improve the pathway over time.

Scalable Learning

The system should be maintainable after launch.

I build pathways with future updates in mind: reusable structures, clear documentation, modular content, consistent naming, reporting standards, and processes that make learning easier to maintain as products, policies, audiences, or business needs change.
