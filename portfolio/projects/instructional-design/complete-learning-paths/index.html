Closed-Loop eLearning Design

Build the experience and the system around it.

A strong eLearning project connects needs analysis, objectives, source content, practice, assessment, development, QA, delivery, and measurement. A course should not become a container for every available slide or document.

For larger pathways, I also plan sequence, prerequisites, certification requirements, LMS behavior, reporting, communications, and ownership so the learner journey works as one experience rather than a collection of disconnected assets.

eLearning Lifecycle

Keep the learning connected from need to update.

The deliverable may be one course or a full pathway, but the workflow should still close the loop.

1

Analyze the Need

Identify the audience, business goal, current performance, constraints, and what the learner actually needs to do differently.

2

Architect the Experience

Align objectives, sequence, format, source content, practice, assessment, supporting resources, and approval points.

3

Develop + Validate

Build efficiently with reusable patterns, accessible design, SME reviews, technical QA, and clear source-file ownership.

4

Deliver + Support

Publish through the right channel or LMS, confirm learner flow and completion logic, and make support resources easy to find.

5

Measure + Update

Use assessment results, analytics, learner feedback, support trends, and stakeholder input to improve the experience over time.

Maintainability

The source files and workflow are part of the deliverable.

I build for the next edit as well as the first launch. Reusable templates, organized assets, documented decisions, editable source files, clear ownership, and assessment or analytics feedback make learning easier to maintain as products, audiences, and business needs change.
