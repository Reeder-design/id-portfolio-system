Overview

How I approach instructional design

I begin with the learner, the performance need, and the business context before choosing a format. The goal is to build the smallest effective solution that supports the required performance.

Depending on the need, that may be a focused interaction, microlearning, multimedia asset, reference resource, assessment, certification pathway, or a connected learning ecosystem.

Performance Need

Start with what someone needs to do.

I separate the performance requirement from the source-content volume, then identify the knowledge, decisions, practice, and support needed to perform well.

Question

What should change in behavior or performance?

Design response

Define scope, objectives, audience needs, and success criteria before selecting a deliverable.

Useful evidence

Stakeholder goals, learner context, task requirements, current gaps, and performance data.
