01eLearningCourses and pathways that connect explanation, practice, assessment, and delivery.RiseStorylineLMS

What changes in this work

I design the full learner path, not just the screen content.

I define what the learner needs to do, organize the source material, decide where practice and assessment belong, build the experience, and prepare it for review and LMS delivery.

1DefineLearner job, audience, source material, scope, and success evidence.
2DesignObjectives, structure, scenarios, assessments, resources, and review points.
3BuildRise, Storyline, custom interactions, accessibility, and QA.
4Deliver + maintainLMS testing, source ownership, updates, and learner feedback.
Example outputA complete learner experience with a clear next action
02MultimediaVideo, motion, audio, and graphics when media explains something better than text alone.VideoMotionAudio

What changes in this work

The production format starts with what the learner needs to see or hear.

I use multimedia when pacing, demonstration, visual sequence, or narration improves the explanation. The work includes scripting, storyboarding, production, editing, accessibility, and reusable source assets.

1DefineWhat needs visual, audio, motion, or demonstration support.
2PlanScript, storyboard, brand requirements, accessibility, and placement.
3ProduceAdobe tools, Camtasia, Colossyan, PowerPoint, or project-specific media tools.
4ReuseEditable source files and modular assets that can be revised later.
Example outputMedia shaped for explanation, demonstration, and learning flow
03Interactive LearningPractice built around a decision, judgment, classification, or response.ScenariosScoringFeedback

What changes in this work

The interaction begins with the decision the learner needs to practice.

I map the inputs, choices, states, scoring, feedback, accessibility, and edge cases before development. I build in Storyline or with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript when the learner task needs more custom behavior.

1DefineThe real decision or judgment the learner needs to practice.
2MapChoices, states, scoring, feedback, accessibility, and edge cases.
3BuildStoryline or browser-based interaction logic and visual states.
4ImproveError patterns and successful interaction patterns inform later scenarios.
Example outputDecision practice with an immediate consequence and explanation
04MicrolearningA short experience for one focused learner need.FocusedModularFast

What changes in this work

I remove everything that does not support the one learner job.

Microlearning works when a product change, concept, judgment, or practice need can stand on its own. I keep the context tight, move quickly into the learner action, and build each piece so it can be updated or reused easily.

1FocusOne thing the learner needs to understand, recognize, explain, or decide.
2Strip backRemove adjacent content and keep only the context needed for the task.
3BuildResponsive modules, short scenarios, demonstrations, and lightweight checks.
4MaintainKeep the module easy to update, sequence, reuse, or retire.
Example outputOne clear learning move without a full course around it
05Performance SupportGuides, tools, references, and resources used in the flow of work.Job aidsToolsResources

What changes in this work

I first decide whether training is actually the right answer.

If the gap is access, process, environment, confidence, or decision support, the better solution may be a guide, repository, dashboard, prompt, or decision tool that sits closer to the work.

1DiagnoseFind the actual source of friction before choosing an intervention.
2MapTask, decision points, source of truth, ownership, and maintenance needs.
3BuildJob aids, references, dashboards, repositories, and decision tools.
4MaintainUse feedback, exceptions, search behavior, and source changes to keep it useful.
Example outputThe answer is available at the point of need
06Learner-facing AIConstrained model-backed experiences designed around a defined learner job.ContextTestingQA

What changes in this work

I design the model behavior and the human learning experience together.

I define the learner task, evidence boundary, instructions, context, allowed behavior, evaluation logic, fallbacks, and review path. The build includes testing weak outputs, refining prompts and context, and maintaining the QA system around the interaction.

1BoundLearner job, risk, evidence, allowed behavior, and human role.
2DesignInstructions, source context, interaction states, fallbacks, and evaluation rules.
3TestPrototype outputs, edge cases, weak responses, and model behavior.
4MaintainInstructions, source material, test cases, evaluation logic, and human review.
Example outputAI behavior constrained around a specific learning task
07Live TrainingFacilitator-led learning that can respond to the room.WorkshopPracticeCoaching

What changes in this work

I design the session and leave room for facilitation.

Live time is most useful for explanation, discussion, questions, practice, and coaching. I plan the objectives, timing, prompts, examples, activities, participant materials, and facilitator guidance, then adapt pacing during delivery.

1PrepareObjectives, timing, content, participant materials, and likely questions.
2StructureExplanations, prompts, polls, examples, activities, and transitions.
3FacilitateGuide discussion and adjust pacing or explanation based on learner response.
4ImproveBring recurring questions and misconceptions into future content updates.
Example outputA planned workshop that still responds to learner needs
08Assessment DesignChecks and certification items aligned to the performance the objective requires.ItemsScoringFeedback

What changes in this work

I start with the evidence of learning, then write the question.

I map objectives to the kind of response the learner needs to demonstrate, then design item coverage, difficulty, distractors, scoring, feedback, remediation, and QA around that evidence.

1DefineThe performance evidence needed for the objective.
2MapItem type, coverage, difficulty, distractors, scoring, and remediation.
3Build + QAItem banks, answer logic, randomization, feedback, and accessible interactions.
4ReviseUse learner errors and item performance to improve weak or outdated questions.
Example outputA question that tests the right level of learner performance
09Performance Tools + ReportingLightweight tools when faster access, analysis, or action solves the real problem.DataWorkflowAction

What changes in this work

I build a tool when another course would add friction.

I map the task, information, decision points, data rules, and review checkpoints, then build a maintainable resource with spreadsheets, reporting tools, HTML, JavaScript, Python, or another fit-for-purpose approach.

1DefineThe user task and the information or decision slowing it down.
2MapInputs, outputs, labels, filters, data rules, and review checkpoints.
3BuildReporting views, spreadsheets, browser tools, or Python-based workflows.
4SupportDocument logic, ownership, exceptions, and source data for future maintenance.
Example outputProgress and exceptions turned into a usable next action
