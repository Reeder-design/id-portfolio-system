How to Use the Demo

Compare the solution target profile with Harborview, then decide how much pursuit investment the evidence supports.

The fictional VitaTrack solution has a target customer profile. Harborview Regional Medical Center is the potential pursuit. Compare the account with the target profile, score the six decision factors, and use the matrix to see whether the evidence supports prioritizing, developing, nurturing, or deprioritizing the opportunity.
