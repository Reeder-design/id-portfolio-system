How to Use the Demo

Compare the solution target profile with Harborview, then decide how much pursuit investment the evidence supports.

The fictional VitaTrack solution has a target customer profile. Harborview Regional Medical Center is the potential pursuit. Compare the account with the target profile, score the six decision factors, and use the matrix to see whether the evidence supports prioritizing, developing, nurturing, or deprioritizing the opportunity.

SOLUTION TARGET PROFILEVitaTrack Mobile Imaging Cart
  • Acute-care hospital with frequent bedside imaging.
  • Portable imaging delays are affecting throughput or staff workflow.
  • Mobile imaging equipment is aging, constrained, or entering replacement planning.
  • Digital imaging environment can support integration after IT and security validation.
POTENTIAL PURSUITHarborview Regional Medical Center
  • 65 portable exams per day and turnaround increased from 24 to 39 minutes.
