Your role: You're a seller for Meridian Medical Systems. Review what has been learned about Harborview Regional Medical Center and decide how strongly the opportunity fits the VitaTrack solution—and how strong the pursuit itself really is.

Part 1 • Understand the Opportunity

Harborview Regional Medical Center

Start with the evidence. The information below represents what the seller has learned through discovery so far.

Fictional Opportunity

Harborview Regional Medical Center

Seller: Meridian Medical Systems • Product: VitaTrack Mobile Imaging Cart

280 Beds Regional acute-care hospital
65 Exams / Day Approximate portable imaging volume
39 Minutes Current average bedside imaging turnaround
7–10 Years Age of existing portable imaging fleet

Operational Situation

  • Four portable imaging units serve the ER, ICU, surgery, and inpatient units.
  • Imaging teams complete roughly 65 portable exams each day.
  • ER and ICU teams frequently wait for a cart to become available.
  • Average bedside imaging turnaround increased from 24 to 39 minutes.
  • Leadership has an active patient-throughput improvement initiative.

Current Environment

  • Harborview already uses a digital PACS environment.
  • Clinical engineering believes VitaTrack integration should be feasible.
  • Formal IT and cybersecurity validation has not occurred.
  • Two aging carts are scheduled for replacement during the next capital cycle.

Stakeholders

  • Director of Radiology is actively advocating for replacement.
  • ER nursing leadership supports improving portable imaging availability.
  • CIO's team has not yet participated.
  • CFO and final capital authority have not been engaged directly.

Buying Situation

  • A preliminary capital request exists.
  • Final funding has not been approved.
  • Harborview is evaluating two competing vendors.
  • Purchase price, clinical workflow, and service support will all matter.

Product Context

VitaTrack Ideal Customer Profile

  • Regional hospitals and hospital systems
  • Approximately 150+ beds
  • High portable imaging volume
  • Multiple departments sharing imaging equipment
  • Existing digital imaging / PACS environment
  • Pressure to reduce imaging delays
  • Aging portable equipment
  • Clinical and IT stakeholders involved in the buying process

Seller Watchlist

Positioning Red Flags

  • No validated clinical workflow problem
  • Very low portable imaging volume
  • Existing equipment was recently replaced
  • Customer is only shopping for lowest price
  • No clinical decision-maker access
  • No capital path or budget visibility
  • IT and security requirements remain unknown
  • Seller begins pitching before validating need
