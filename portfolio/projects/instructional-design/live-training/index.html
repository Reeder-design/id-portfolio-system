Facilitation Foundation

Live adaptation started before corporate L&D.

Before moving into instructional design, I spent three years teaching high-school history and English. That experience built the habits I still use in live learning: explain clearly, check understanding, and change the approach when the room needs something different.

Live Training Work

Two parts of my role

I either build the session and facilitator materials, facilitate the session, or contribute to both parts of the work.

Content Development

Build the session, materials, and facilitator guidance.

I create virtual or in-person training content with the objectives, pacing, presentation content, activities, participant materials, prompts, and facilitator notes needed for delivery.

Structure the session

Define objectives, agenda, pacing, transitions, activities, and debriefs around what learners need to do.

Build the materials

Create presentation content, facilitator notes, participant resources, polls, prompts, handouts, and shared references.

Add practice

Use discussion, scenarios, activities, and guided questions so learners apply the content during the session.

Prepare for delivery

Document timing, instructions, transitions, resources, and backup plans for the facilitator.
