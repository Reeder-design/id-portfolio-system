Project Example

Facilitating Live Sales Training

I supported a multi-session virtual sales workshop by preparing assigned content, facilitating decision-focused modules, supporting the virtual room, and using learner questions to improve future learning ideas.

Prepared the content

I attended the workshop first as a learner and observer, reviewed facilitator resources, and focused preparation on the modules I was assigned to teach.

Facilitated live modules

I presented assigned decision-focused content, prompted participation, used questions and discussion, and connected the material to examples raised by experienced sellers.

Used delivery as research

I tracked recurring questions and learner challenges, then turned those observations into ideas for stronger scenarios and assessment questions without retaining private learner details.
