Read the Room

I changed the delivery when the room gave me a different signal.

Choose a learner signal. The whole state shows what I read, what I changed live, and what that signal taught me about the learning itself.

Live room signal
Mixed confidence
What I read

The concept or distinction was not stable yet.

What I did next

I slowed down, reframed the concept, and tested it again with a concrete seller example.

What came back to design

A low-confidence response showed where future explanations, examples, or formative checks needed to make the distinction clearer.
