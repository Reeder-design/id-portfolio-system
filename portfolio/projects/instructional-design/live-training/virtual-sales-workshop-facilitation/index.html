Design Feedback

What live delivery taught me about the content

I used recurring questions and friction from the sessions as input for later self-paced content and facilitator guidance.

Questions + misconceptions

Repeated questions showed where explanations, examples, distractors, or formative feedback needed to be clearer.

Seller examples

I noted examples that made the framework concrete and transferable across internal and partner audiences.

Timing

I noted sections that took too long so future sessions could protect more time for application and debrief.
