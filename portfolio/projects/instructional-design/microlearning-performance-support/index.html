Choosing the right training

What does the person need to do differently?

The answer tells me whether the work belongs in learning, at the point of need, or in a connected combination.

Use microlearning

Build focused context or give someone a short chance to practice.

Microlearning fits when the learner needs a new mental model, product context, or judgment before the next task. It may include practice, but it does not need to become a complete certification.

Best signal
Performance depends on understanding or judgment.
Typical output
Short module, scenario, demonstration, or guided practice.

How they work together

Learning prepares the person. Support stays with the work.

A short learning experience can introduce the situation and let someone practice. The connected job aid, dashboard, guide, or repository remains available when the task happens for real.

Before the task

Build the model

During the task

Support the work

Put the answer, tool, or reference where it can be used immediately.

After the task

Make return easy

Keep takeaways and deeper resources findable after the lesson is over.
