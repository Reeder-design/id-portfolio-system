Instructional design · focused learning

Microlearning for the moment that matters.

I design short learning experiences around one job-relevant move: what someone needs to understand, decide, or do next. The work is in choosing the right scope, giving learners a useful action, and making the result easy to carry back into the job.

The design test

Small in scope. Complete in purpose.

A short runtime alone does not make a module microlearning. I look for four qualities that let a learner finish a useful piece of work without carrying the whole course with them.

01

One performance outcome

Name the action the learner should be able to take, then work backward from it.

02

Only the context needed

Keep the explanation and visual framing that support that action. Move deeper detail to a maintained resource.

03

An active learning move

Ask learners to recognize a cue, make a choice, or rehearse a response when the objective calls for judgment.

04

A usable handoff

Give feedback, a clear takeaway, or a resource learners can return to as the work continues.

How long? Long enough to accomplish the focused outcome, and no longer. I choose length after the task, context, and practice are clear.

Choose the learning response

The format follows the learner need.

Common forms · my work

Different moments call for different small experiences.

These are the formats I have used in the work shown below. Each begins with a specific learner need, not a preset duration or template.

Orient

Product or process change

A concise introduction makes the new offer, its value, and its boundaries clear before a conversation.

My approach I translated approved launch material into a Rise overview, then kept deeper details in linked resources.

Practice

Decision or conversation

One realistic cue asks the learner to choose a response and receive feedback on the reasoning.

My approach I paired the product-launch overview with Storyline scenario practice focused on customer fit and the next move.

Contextualize

Audience or vertical focus

A shared learning pattern keeps navigation familiar while the examples and judgments fit a specific audience.

My approach I built one Rise module with separate seaport and airport paths, scenario practice, selective media, and specialist review.
