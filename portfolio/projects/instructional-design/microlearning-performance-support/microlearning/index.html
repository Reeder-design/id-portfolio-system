Instructional Design / Content Type

Microlearning

I treat microlearning as its own instructional design format: a compact module built around one useful learner job, enough context to act, and only the practice or support needed for that moment.

Focused ModulesProduct ChangeDecision PracticeModular Design

Development + design approach

Design the smallest complete learning experience that can do the job.

I start with one learner outcome, remove content that belongs somewhere else, and decide what combination of explanation, visual framing, practice, feedback, and takeaways will move the learner forward without turning the module into a compressed full course.

A product or process changed

Give the learner the context they need before the next conversation.

Cover what changed, what matters, and where to go for the rest.

Microlearning as a strategy

Why have a microlearning category?

Microlearning is not a smaller version of a complete learning pathway. It is built for a different moment.

What does the learner need right now?

Complete learning pathway

Use a pathway when people need a broader capability over time.

Pathways can connect several lessons, guided practice, and assessment. They are the right choice when the learning goal is more than one quick, contextual need.

Good for
Building a durable capability across several skills.
