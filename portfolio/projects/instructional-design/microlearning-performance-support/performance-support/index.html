Point-of-need design

Performance Support

I use performance support when work is happening and the next action depends on a reliable answer. I trace the friction, check the source with the right owner, and put guidance where a person will actually use it.

The strategy helps someone find, interpret, apply, or act at the moment of need. A guide, response template, dashboard, or job aid is one possible output.

Performance consulting lens

Look through the problem before choosing the support.

A request for training can hide an access, process, source, system, or exception problem. I test the cause first, then match the response and the owner.

Purpose before format

Different forms of L&D performance support.

The format follows the task, the source, and the person who must keep the answer current. These public examples show reusable work patterns without private learner or system details.

01 / QUICK ACTION

Decision prompts and reusable responses

When the same question interrupts live work, I organize the answer around the trigger, source check, next action, and exception route. This can become a prompt, checklist, or response template.

Use when
A person needs a reliable next step.
Grounded in
Issue categories, access guidance, and support templates.
02 / MAINTAINED SOURCE

Guides, repositories, and operational references

Some answers need more context than a prompt can hold. I map a request to the information needed, a reusable response, and an escalation route, then make the authoritative source and update owner visible.

Use when
Accuracy and source consistency matter.
Grounded in
Source guides, incoming-information guides, and maintained references.
03 / LIVE STATUS

Dashboards and reporting views

When the next action depends on current status, a reporting view can surface what needs follow-up. I design the labels, filters, source rules, and exception cues so the view supports a decision rather than just displaying data.

Use when
Status or exceptions change with the work.
Grounded in
LMS reporting and operational follow-up patterns.
04 / CONVERSATION

Talk tracks, decks, and manager resources

Sometimes the work is a conversation. I turn approved source information into a short sequence of cues, questions, and next steps that a facilitator, manager, or seller can use without reading a full guide aloud.

Use when
Someone needs to guide a live discussion.
Grounded in
Enablement tools and program support resources.
05 / CONNECTED LEARNING

A resource that outlives the lesson

A short lesson can build context and practice; a maintained resource supplies the answer when the real task arrives. I keep changing details outside the course and give the learner a clear path back to them.

Use when
Preparation and repeated application both matter.
Grounded in
Launch learning with linked, current resources.
Explore the separate Microlearning page →
06 / EXCEPTION ROUTE

Triage and human handoff

Self-service has a boundary. For access, data, system, or unusual cases, I separate the known path from the exception and show what context an administrator, SME, or support owner needs to continue.

Use when
The resource cannot resolve the case alone.
Grounded in
Issue-to-response guides and change workflows.

These visuals are public-safe demonstrations of the support logic. Private cases, learner records, support threads, and system details are omitted.
