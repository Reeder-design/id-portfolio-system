What the real project demonstrates

Two assets. One launch-readiness goal.

I kept product orientation and decision practice separate so approved messaging could change without rebuilding the scenario, and the practice could be revised when customer conversations revealed a new need.

Scope the source

I filtered launch and product-marketing material to what sellers needed to explain, recognize, or act on.

Build the practice

I used a focused customer scenario and explanatory feedback to rehearse the next-best move.

Review and revise

I coordinated SME and QA feedback in Review 360 and tracked changes in Asana, including navigation, feedback, and completion messaging.
