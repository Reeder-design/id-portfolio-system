My decision

What it produced

Outcome

Concise launch information plus practice sellers could apply.

Focused overview

Delivered positioning, customer-fit signals, and supporting resources in a format sellers could scan quickly.

Applied practice

Moved beyond passive product review by asking sellers to make a customer-facing decision and learn from explanatory feedback.

Modular maintenance

Kept overview and practice assets separate so changing launch guidance did not require rebuilding the entire experience.
