The Need

Prepare sellers for launch without building more course than they needed.

The approved source material contained more product detail than a seller needed before a customer conversation. I narrowed the learning to the customer problem, value, likely fit, and next step, then created separate practice for the judgment piece.

Scope

Reduce launch material to the information sellers needed to explain or recognize.

Practice

Give sellers a customer-facing decision instead of another content screen.

Maintenance

Keep launch context and practice independent so either asset could change without a full rebuild.

Two-Asset Design

One asset explained the launch. The other let sellers practice the decision.

My decision

What it produced

A scoped source set organized around customer problem, value, fit, and resources.

Review + Refinement

Review changed the instructions, feedback, navigation, and completion experience.

Instructions

I tightened directions where the learner action was not immediately obvious.

Feedback

I revised scenario feedback so it explained the reasoning behind each choice instead of only marking it right or wrong.

Navigation + completion

I improved readability, navigation, and completion messaging so the learner ended with a clear next step.

Outcome

Two assets, each with one job.

Rise overview

Delivered the launch context in a concise, scannable format.

Storyline scenario

Moved sellers from passive product review into a customer-facing decision with feedback.

Independent updates

Positioning and practice could be revised separately as launch guidance changed.

Public-safe case study: product names, proprietary launch messaging, internal screenshots, customer details, reviewer comments, and source materials are omitted.
