Launch Strategy

Give sellers enough product fluency to move the conversation forward.

This was sales enablement, not technical certification. The learning had to help sellers describe the offer accurately, recognize customer-fit signals, and take an appropriate next step without overloading them with implementation detail.

Design Outcome

A compact launch package, not a product encyclopedia.

The design concentrated on the decisions sellers needed to make and kept the learning modular enough to review and maintain as launch guidance changed.

No invented performance metrics: this case focuses on the design result and workflow rather than publishing internal learner data or proprietary launch measurements.

Seller-ready

Prepare for the conversation, not a technical exam.

The learner could review what the offer solves, recognize fit signals, and rehearse a customer-facing next step without needing implementation-level depth.

Design evidenceRise overview + scenario-based Storyline practice

Public-safe case study: proprietary product names, launch messaging, internal screenshots, customer details, reviewer comments, and source materials are omitted. DraftPath is a sanitized replacement for confidential launch content.
