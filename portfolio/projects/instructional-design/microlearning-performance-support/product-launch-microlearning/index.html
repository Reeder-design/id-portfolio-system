Design Need

Give sellers enough product fluency to move the conversation forward.

This was sales enablement, not technical certification. The learning had to help sellers describe the offer accurately, recognize customer-fit signals, and take an appropriate next step without overloading them with implementation detail.

THE LAUNCH REQUESTHelp sellers explain the new offer and recognize when a customer needs it.

Seller task 1 · Position

Connect the product to a clear customer value story.

The design started with the conversation sellers needed to have: explain what the offer solves, who it is for, and why the customer should care without drifting into implementation detail.
