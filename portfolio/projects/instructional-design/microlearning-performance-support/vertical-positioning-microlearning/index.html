01

Need

What needed to change?

Help sellers build usable industry context quickly while avoiding a broad vertical course that would be too long for the moment of need.

Audience

Internal sellers and channel partners who need concise industry context before discovery and positioning conversations.

What learners needed to do

  • Recognize the operating context and common priorities within each industry setting.
  • Connect customer needs to relevant solution use cases at a sales-ready level.
  • Practice making a customer-facing decision in a short scenario.
  • Locate supporting resources for deeper follow-up instead of overloading the lesson.
02

Design Decisions

How I shaped the solution

Approach

Split the original scope into separate industry modules so each experience could stay focused and short. Reuse a consistent pattern of context, use cases, practice, and resources, and use multimedia only where it supported the learning rather than turning the lesson into a video-first experience.

03

Build

How I developed and validated it

Built two Rise modules designed to stay under seven minutes, added scenario practice and a short AI-presenter video as one supporting element, and coordinated internal QA plus specialist review for each industry area. The structure was refined when the original combined scope proved too broad.

04

Outcome

What came out of the work?

  • Turned one broad vertical topic into two focused modules with clearer boundaries and shorter learner paths.
  • Created a repeatable short-form pattern that connects context, use cases, practice, and follow-up resources.
  • Used specialist review and scoped multimedia to keep the content useful without adding unnecessary depth.
