Production Workflow

Media decisions start with the learning need.

I do not add multimedia just to make a course look busy. I choose the medium based on what needs to be explained, demonstrated, reinforced, or remembered.

Pre-production

Plan the message before opening the production tool.

I define the purpose, script, storyboard, screen or shot plan, visual hierarchy, and where the finished asset belongs in the learner journey.

Decision

Multimedia in the design process

Media supports the learning decision, not the other way around.

Most original media from client and employer work cannot be published in a public portfolio, so I sanitize or remove it from project examples. This section explains how I use multimedia in the design and development process without presenting confidential production assets as public samples.

Explain

Use motion, narration, or visual sequencing when they make a complex idea easier to understand than text alone.

Orient

Use short video, presenter media, or branded visuals to establish context before the learner moves into practice.

Support practice

Pair media with a learner action so visuals lead into comparison, scenario work, feedback, or another meaningful decision.

Refine + maintain

Caption, edit, QA, modularize, and retain source files so the media can be updated as the learning changes.

Public portfolio noteProject pages may describe the role multimedia played or show reconstructed and sanitized visuals. They do not reproduce confidential source media.
