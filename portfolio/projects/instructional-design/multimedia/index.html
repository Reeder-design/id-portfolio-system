Choose the Job

What does the media need to do?

Explain a concept without adding more reading.

I use concise visuals, narration, motion, or an AI presenter when a short explanation can carry the idea more efficiently than another block of text.

How I Use Multimedia

Production is part of the learning design, not decoration after the fact.

I decide what the learner needs to see, hear, or do before I decide which production tool to open.

Start with the learning job

Media earns its place by explaining, demonstrating, reinforcing, or supporting practice.

Control the signal

I use pacing, visual hierarchy, audio, and motion to direct attention instead of competing for it.

Design for reuse

I keep media modular when possible so one update does not force a rebuild of the entire learning experience.
