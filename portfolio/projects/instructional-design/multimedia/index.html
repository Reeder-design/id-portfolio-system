Instructional Design

Multimedia Training Content

I use multimedia when it helps make information easier to understand, demonstrate, remember, or apply. The goal is to choose the right combination of visuals, audio, motion, and interaction for the learning need.

Design Approach

Use the medium that best supports the message.

Multimedia can help learners see a process, hear an explanation, watch a demonstration, compare information, or understand something that would be harder to communicate through text alone.

I consider the purpose of each media element, how much information the learner needs at once, and whether the experience remains clear, accessible, and easy to use.

What I Consider

Media should support learning.

  • Learning objective and audience
  • Information hierarchy
  • Visual clarity
  • Narration and pacing
  • Accessibility
  • Cognitive load
  • Reusability across learning formats

Multimedia Workflow

Start with the learning need, not the production tool.

1

Define the Purpose

Determine what the learner needs to understand, see, hear, or practice.

2

Plan the Experience

Structure the content, script, visuals, pacing, narration, and learner flow.

3

Produce the Media

Create and edit the visual, audio, video, animation, or interactive assets.

4

QA the Experience

Review content accuracy, pacing, accessibility, functionality, and overall usability.
