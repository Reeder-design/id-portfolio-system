Production Workflow

Media decisions start with the learning need.

I do not add multimedia just to make a course look busy. I choose the medium based on what needs to be explained, demonstrated, reinforced, or remembered.

Pre-production

Plan the message before opening the production tool.

I define the purpose, script, storyboard, screen or shot plan, visual hierarchy, and where the finished asset belongs in the learner journey.

Decision

Choose the medium only when it supports the learning objective or learner task.

Output

Script, storyboard, production notes, and a clear role for the asset.

QA lens

Check scope, length, accessibility needs, and maintenance expectations before production begins.
