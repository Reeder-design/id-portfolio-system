Learner Journey

Follow what has to work from discovery through follow-up.

A strong course can still fail if learners cannot find it, enroll correctly, launch it, receive completion credit, or know what comes next.

Find

Make the right learning easy to discover.

Instructional DesignUse clear titles, descriptions, prerequisites, outcomes, and supporting resources so learners understand what the content is for.
Learner SignalSearch behavior, wrong-course launches, support questions, and repeated requests for links show where discovery is breaking down.

Operational Signals

What tells me the learner path needs attention.

  • Support tickets can reveal confusing instructions or broken learner paths.
  • Launch and completion patterns can expose compatibility or UX friction.
  • Enrollment exceptions can reveal unclear audience or pathway logic.
  • Reporting gaps can show where tracking or content structure needs revision.
