LMS Administration & System Operations

The learning experience starts before a course opens. I work on the platform structures, enrollment logic, content operations, support processes, reporting, and system decisions that determine whether learning is easy to find, launch, complete, measure, and maintain.

Explore the Work All Projects

Two Perspectives

The platform and the learning experience are one system

I approach LMS work from both the administrator and operator side and the instructional-design side. The system has to work for the people maintaining it and for the learner moving through it.

Admin & Operations Lens

Make the platform predictable to operate

I look at how configuration, structure, support, reporting, documentation, and governance affect the people responsible for running the learning environment.

  • Platform UX and administrative workflows
  • User support and issue resolution
  • Content structures, enrollment logic, and ownership
  • Documentation, migration, reporting, and governance
  • Working within platform constraints without creating unnecessary learner friction

Instructional Design Lens

Make the learner journey make sense

I also look at how the LMS changes the experience of the learning itself, including what learners see, how content launches, how pathways connect, and how feedback reaches the design team.

  • Learner UX across discovery, enrollment, launch, and completion
  • Authoring-tool and content compatibility
  • Pathways, prerequisites, certifications, and reinforcement
  • User feedback and support patterns as design signals
  • Analytics and platform behavior that influence content decisions

Operating Principle

Reduce friction without hiding complexity

The goal is not to make the platform look simple by pushing complexity onto administrators or learners. Good system design puts complexity in the right place, documents it clearly, and makes routine actions dependable.

System Operations

What this work includes

Structure & Governance

Roles, permissions, content organization, ownership, naming, lifecycle rules, and repeatable administrative processes.

Pathways & Delivery

Enrollments, prerequisites, certifications, content compatibility, launch behavior, and the learner path through the system.

Support & Documentation

User support, troubleshooting, administrator guidance, process documentation, and clear handoffs across teams.

Reporting & Change

Reporting quality, migration planning, validation, data cleanup, platform transitions, and evidence for continuous improvement.
