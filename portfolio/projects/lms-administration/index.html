Learner Journey

I test the path, not just the course.

The LMS has to work from discovery through completion and follow-up, especially across different employee, partner, and customer audiences.

Find

Make the right learning easy to discover.

System / AdminStructure catalogs, audiences, search, visibility, naming, and permissions so the right content appears for the right people.
Instructional DesignUse clear titles, descriptions, prerequisites, outcomes, and supporting resources so learners understand what the content is for.
Learner SignalSearch behavior, wrong-course launches, support questions, and repeated requests for links show where discovery is breaking down.

Operational Signals

What tells me the path needs attention.

  • Support requests reveal confusing instructions or broken learner paths.
  • Launch and completion patterns expose compatibility or tracking issues.
  • Enrollment exceptions point to audience or pathway problems.
  • Reporting gaps show where the structure or process needs revision.
