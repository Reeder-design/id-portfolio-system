What was changing

The learning ecosystem was moving into a new LMS.

Moving courses was one part of the transition. I helped check that learners could find the right content, follow connected learning plans, complete certifications, and return for renewals.

Catalogs, visibility, navigation, support guidance, and reporting expectations also needed attention. I tested the learner experience alongside the structures behind it.
