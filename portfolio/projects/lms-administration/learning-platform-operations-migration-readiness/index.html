My Migration Scope

My scope was the content and learner-experience layer of the Absorb-to-Docebo migration.

Because I already administered learning content in Absorb, I could compare known courses, learning plans, certification behavior, learner expectations, and support patterns against the migrated Docebo experience.

I focused on where the learner journey could break: content relationships, audience visibility, certification and renewal behavior, navigation, completion expectations, and guidance around the new platform.

Legacy Platform / Absorb LMS

I started from the learning ecosystem we were already operating in Absorb.

My migration work depended on understanding the existing courses, learning plans, certifications, catalogs, learner expectations, and support patterns before checking how those experiences translated into Docebo.

What I brought forward

Knowledge of existing content, learner journeys, certifications, and support workflows.

What could not be assumed

That relationships, visibility, renewals, or learner navigation would behave the same way in the new LMS.

Why it mattered

The legacy experience gave me a baseline for identifying what changed, broke, duplicated, or became confusing.
