Build one learning system for multiple audiences.

Absorb demonstrates daily course operations. Docebo shows how branded partner environments and delegated administration can scale without losing control.

04 / Knowledge portal

Govern answers people need during work.

SharePoint Sites supports searchable job aids and current guidance. The demo focuses on ownership, versioning, access, and publishing rather than course completion.
