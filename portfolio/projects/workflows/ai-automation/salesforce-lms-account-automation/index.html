Operational Need

Reduce repetitive account research and LMS setup without hiding uncertain records.

The original process required repeated Salesforce searches, copying account information into Excel, translating that data into LMS department fields, and preparing learner moves. I automated the repeatable steps while keeping no-match and ambiguous cases visible for review.

Repeated lookup work

Use each learner email to search Salesforce and capture the account record needed for downstream setup.

Consistent field mapping

Translate Salesforce account values into the department fields Absorb needs without rebuilding the logic each time.

Visible exceptions

Mark missing or uncertain matches for human review instead of treating every search result as automatically correct.
