Overview

Workflow design for learning operations

I use documented workflows to make design decisions, handoffs, reviews, and development easier to follow and repeat.

I also automate repetitive work, connect content with LMS and reporting systems, and use AI where it improves speed or quality without removing review and ownership.

Documentation, versioning, analytics, and clear ownership keep learning products maintainable after launch and create evidence for continuous improvement.

Reduce friction

Remove manual steps and unclear handoffs that do not add useful judgment.

Keep review visible

Automate repeatable mechanics while preserving human review for quality, confidentiality, and final decisions.

Design for maintenance

Build documentation, ownership, reporting, and reuse into the workflow before launch.
