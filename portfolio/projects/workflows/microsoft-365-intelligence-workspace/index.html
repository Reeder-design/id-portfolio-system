Microsoft 365 · Learning + Intelligence

One workspace. Connected decisions.

I connected a multi-page SharePoint site, role-based training routes, an Intelligence Hub, and content health workflows into a working Microsoft 365 experience. People can move from the right learning resource to a current market snapshot and a reviewable next action.

The connected system

Give each person a useful starting point.

The SharePoint site brings together pages for training, tool access, and intelligence. Role-specific routes link to Highspot training for the relevant tools and access levels; the Intelligence Hub connects reporting, market views, and cross-team updates; content health workflows turn source material into signals that can be reviewed and acted on.

Content health · automated intake

Turn source material into a reviewable signal.

Connected workflows take meeting recordings and other source content into content health checks and analytics. The output is a visible review path, helping teams identify what needs attention and keep the human decision in the loop.

  • Bring recordings and source content into the flow
  • Surface health signals and supporting evidence
  • Route findings toward review and follow-up
